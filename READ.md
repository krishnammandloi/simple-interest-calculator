# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple web-based calculator that calculates Simple Interest and Total Amount.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

## Features

- Enter principal amount
- Enter rate of interest
- Enter time in years
- Calculate simple interest
- Calculate total amount
- Simple and user-friendly interface

## Technologies Used

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript

## How to Use

1. Enter the principal amount.
2. Enter the rate of interest.
3. Enter the time in years.
4. Click the Calculate button.
5. The Simple Interest and Total Amount will be displayed.

## Example

Principal = ₹10,000  
Rate = 5%  
Time = 2 years

Simple Interest = ₹1,000

Total Amount = ₹11,000
